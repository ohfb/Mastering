What the app actually does

It uses on-device rhythm and energy analysis, transient mapping, likely tempo and meter estimation, vocal-activity detection, adaptive EQ, cleanup, compression, transient shaping, stereo width, soft saturation, limiting, tempo adjustment, and 24-bit WAV encoding. Apple Intelligence itself is only available to native Swift apps, so this web version uses private browser-side DSP and analysis.
